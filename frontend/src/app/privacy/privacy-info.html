What LeoWay reads from WebUntis

  • Your timetable and the school's period times, to know your first lesson.
  • Cancelled and changed lessons, so the leave-by time moves with them.
  • Date and time of your absence and lateness entries, so we can match them with delays.
  • Exam dates, for exam warnings. Never grades.

What LeoWay never stores

  • Your WebUntis password. You log in with an access key instead, which you can revoke in WebUntis at any time.
  • Absence reasons, notes or excuses.
  • Your location (no GPS) or your home address.
  • Messages, homework or grades: LeoWay cannot request them.

How your access key is protected

The key is stored encrypted on the LeoWay server. The encryption key is kept separately, outside the database and the source code. The key is never written to logs.

How long data is kept

  • Your account, saved routes and contacts: until you delete your account.
  • Timetable data: replaced on every sync.
  • Lateness entries and appeals: until the end of the school year or until you delete your account.
  • Bus and tram delay records are not personal data and are kept for statistics.

Your choice

Using LeoWay is voluntary. If you are 14 or older, you can agree yourself. You can disconnect WebUntis or delete your account at any time under Route and alerts; deleting removes your key, routes and lateness entries.

LeoWay is a school project at HTL Leonding. Questions: ask the LeoWay team.
